# Homepage Showcase Redesign

## Goal
Rebuild the homepage as an original 74HRS showcase using the reference's long-form rhythm and presentation format, without copying its content or graphics.

## What will change
- Keep the existing video opening and restore the original navigation treatment.
- Reorder the page into a focused story: video, client/impact strip, selected work, creative process, services, products, VIP previews, testimonials, closing call-to-action, FAQ, and footer.
- Use alternating full-width showcases, compact grids, product previews, and editorial feature blocks inspired by the reference layout.
- Preserve the 74HRS identity, existing portfolio/shop data, links, booking flow, VIP behavior, and account controls.
- Replace unfinished styling and media gaps with intentional, clearly labeled placeholders that can be swapped later.
- Make every section work cleanly on mobile and desktop with restrained motion.

## Visual direction
- Dark 74HRS canvas with sharper white type, silver surfaces, and controlled neon accent moments.
- Compact navigation, generous breathing room, rounded preview frames, and dense but readable content groups.
- Original compositions and copy tailored to VFX, GFX, digital assets, and creator communities.

## Technical details
- Consolidate homepage styling around semantic theme tokens.
- Rework homepage section components without changing business logic or routes.
- Remove invalid remote font imports and use the project's local font stack.
- Validate the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation and key links.
